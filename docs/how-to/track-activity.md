# How to Track an Activity

This guide covers all the ways to track activities in CareKeeper, from simple one-tap tracking to detailed entries with notes and locations.

## Quick Steps (iPhone)

1. Open your caree's detail view
2. Tap Track Activity
3. Tap an activity type
4. Adjust quantity/time if needed
5. Tap **Save**

## Quick Steps (Watch)

1. Open CareKeeper on your watch
2. Tap your caree
3. Tap Track Activity
4. Tap an activity type button
5. Adjust quantity with Digital Crown if needed
6. Tap **Save** or press crown

## Tracking on iPhone

### Basic Activity (No Quantity/Location)

For simple tracking like diaper changes or naps:

1. Open your caree's detail view
2. Tap the activity type (e.g., "Diaper Change")
3. The tracking form appears with time set to "Now"
4. Tap **Save**

[SCREENSHOT: Simple activity tracking form]


### Activity with Quantity

For tracking amounts (feeding, water, etc.):

1. Tap the activity type (e.g., "Bottle")
2. Tap the quantity field and enter the amount using the number pad
3. The unit is shown automatically (e.g., "oz")
4. Tap **Save**

[SCREENSHOT: Quantity entry with number pad]

!!! tip "Quick Entry"
    The number pad includes a decimal point for precise tracking (e.g., "3.5 oz").

### Activity with Location

For activities with location tracking enabled:

1. Tap the activity type (e.g., "Walk")
2. CareKeeper automatically captures your current location
3. You'll see "📍 Location captured" or a loading indicator
4. Enter quantity if applicable
5. Tap **Save**

[SCREENSHOT: Activity with location capture]

!!! note "Location Permission"
    CareKeeper needs location permission to capture locations. Grant permission in Settings > CareKeeper > Location if prompted.

!!! warning "Location Capture After Granting Permission"
Location may not be captured immediately after granting location permissions. Toggle the location toggle off and back on again. On a phone, you should see lat and long coordinates. On a watch, you should see “Location captured” or a similar message 

### Customizing Date and Time

To track an activity that happened earlier:

1. Tap the **Date/Time** field
2. Use the date picker to select:
   - Date (scroll to select day)
   - Time (scroll hours and minutes)
3. Tap outside the picker to confirm
4. Complete the rest of the form
5. Tap **Save**

[SCREENSHOT: Date/Time picker]

!!! example "Backfilling Data"
    Useful when you forgot to track something earlier: "Oh, I fed her at 2pm but forgot to log it."

### Adding Notes

Notes let you record additional details:

1. Fill in the activity as normal
2. Tap the **Notes** field
3. Type any relevant information:
4. Tap **Save**

[SCREENSHOT: Notes field with keyboard]

!!! tip "Notes Are Optional"
    Try to structure your Activity Types so that most activities don't need notes (info captured in activity type name)

## Tracking on Apple Watch

The watch app is optimized for speed—perfect for busy moments.

### Opening the Watch App

1. Raise your wrist or press the Digital Crown
2. Find and tap the CareKeeper icon
3. You'll see your caree list

[SCREENSHOT: Watch app caree list]

### Simple One-Tap Tracking

For activities without quantity:

1. Tap your caree
2. Tap the activity type button
3. You'll see a checkmark confirmation
4. Activity is tracked immediately!

[SCREENSHOT: Watch showing activity confirmation]

!!! success "2-Second Tracking"
    Perfect for quick moments: diaper change while holding baby, quick nap tracking, etc.

### Tracking with Quantity (Digital Crown)

For activities with quantity:

1. Tap your caree
2. Tap the activity type button
3. The quantity screen appears
4. Turn the **Digital Crown** to adjust:
   - Clockwise: increase
   - Counterclockwise: decrease
   - Faster spin: larger jumps
5. Tap **Save** or press the crown

[SCREENSHOT: Watch quantity adjustment with Digital Crown highlighted]

!!! tip "Digital Crown Control"
    The Digital Crown is the best way to adjust values on Apple Watch—much easier than trying to type!

### Custom Time on Watch

By default, activities are tracked at the current time. To change:

1. After selecting activity type, swipe down
2. Toggle **Custom Time** to ON
3. Use the Digital Crown to scroll the time
4. Tap **Save**

[SCREENSHOT: Watch custom time selection]

!!! tip "Make sure time input is selected"
    When you are setting both a quantity and a custom time, tap the time input to make sure it is selected. Otherwise, you may accidentally adjust the quantity instead.

## Editing Activities

You can edit activities after tracking:

### On iPhone

1. Open the activity list
2. Tap on the activity
3. Tap **Edit** from the toolbar
4. Modify quantity, time, notes, or location
5. Tap **Save**

[SCREENSHOT: Swipe left to edit activity]

Alternatively:
1. Tap the activity to open detail view
2. Tap **Edit** in the top-right
3. Make changes
4. Tap **Save**

[SCREENSHOT: Activity detail view with edit button]

### On Watch

1. Open the activity list
2. Tap the activity
3. Tap **Edit**
4. Use Digital Crown to adjust quantity or time
5. Tap **Save**

[SCREENSHOT: Watch activity edit view]

## Deleting Activities

### On iPhone

2. Swipe left on the activity

4. Confirm deletion

[SCREENSHOT: Swipe left to delete]

### On Watch

1. Open the activity list
2. Tap the activity
3. Scroll down and tap **Delete Activity**

[SCREENSHOT: Watch delete confirmation]

### Location Not Capturing
- Check that location permission is granted in Settings
- Make sure you're not in airplane mode
- Try toggling "Has Location" off and on for the activity type

### Wrong Time Showing
- Check your device's time zone settings
- CareKeeper uses your device's current time
- Edit the activity to correct the time

### Activity Not Syncing to Other Devices
- Check internet connection
- Pull down to refresh activity list
- Check sync status indicator in settings

### Cannot Edit Activity
- Make sure you're the creator or owner
- Check that you have internet connection (for shared activities)

For more help, see the [Troubleshooting Guide](../reference/troubleshooting.md).

## Best Practices

### Quick Tracking Tips
- ⌚ Use watch for frequent activities (feeding, diaper changes)
- 📱 Use iPhone for activities needing notes or editing
- 🕐 Track immediately rather than trying to remember later
- 📝 Only add notes when relevant (don't force it)

### Accuracy Tips
- Use custom time for activities you forgot to track

### Efficiency Tips
- Reorder activity types to put frequent ones first
- Create keyboard shortcuts for common notes phrases (iOS Settings > General > Keyboard > Text Replacement)
- Use watch complications for instant access
